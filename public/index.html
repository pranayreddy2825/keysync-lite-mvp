AI Brain · How KeySync thinks

A single brain orchestrating Gemini, Qdrant and lead scoring for Dubai brokerages.

1. Capture

Leads arrive from WhatsApp, email or portals. KeySync normalises the text, strips signatures and noise, and tags the channel.

2. Understand (Gemini)

Gemini analyses intent (rent / buy), budget, timeframe, location hints and whether it's an end-user or investor. We convert this into a structured lead profile.

3. Recall (Qdrant)

The profile is embedded and used to query Qdrant for Dubai-specific snippets: areas, personas, FAQs, and brokerage playbook rules.

4. Decide & Route

A scoring layer decides whether AI should reply or escalate to a broker. The reply is persona-aware and cites the snippets used.

Why this matters

Judges & investors see that KeySync isn't “just a chatbot” – it's a triage layer that protects high-value deals while compressing response time for the rest to seconds.
